# Prime Video Content Strategy Analysis


##  Objective

The goal of this project is to analyze Prime Video content and understand how content type, genre, and other factors affect quality and performance.



## Dataset

* Dataset contains ~10,000 titles from Prime Video
* Features include: genre, type (movie/show), ratings, release year, runtime, etc.


## Tools Used

* Python
* Pandas
* Matplotlib & Seaborn


## Key Insights

* Content has increased a lot over time, but average ratings stayed almost the same  more content is not equalto better quality

* Movies make up around 86% of the platform, but TV shows have higher average ratings (~7 vs ~5.8)

* Most content is concentrated in genres like Drama and Comedy, but these are not the highest-rated genres

* Genres like Documentary and History have better ratings but less content → opportunity for improvement

* Mature content (TV-MA, TV-14) generally performs better than general audience content

* Movies show high variation in ratings, while TV shows are more consistent



##  Conclusion

Overall, the platform focuses more on quantity and popular genres, but better performance comes from TV shows, niche genres, and mature content.



##  Key Takeaway

High-volume content does not always mean high performance.
Better results come from focusing on quality and the right type of content.
